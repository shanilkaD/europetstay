Privacy

How EuroPetStay handles information when you use this website.

Information we receive

When you sign in, we receive the account information needed to identify you. If you use Google sign-in, Google may provide your name, email address, and profile photo. If you request an email sign-in link, we use your email address to send it. If you use the service, we also store the profile details, listing text and photos, and messages you provide.

How information is used and shown

We use this information to operate accounts, display pet-sitting listings, help owners and sitters communicate, and maintain the service. Listing details, listing photos, and profile display names may be visible to website visitors. Conversations are available to their participants.

Private safety information

If you create a sitter post, you may be asked to upload an identity document and provide a reference’s name and contact details. These items are stored in private Supabase storage and tables and are not displayed on public listings. The current website has no in-app ID review process; uploading a document does not verify your identity. Veterinary and emergency contact information is stored separately from public listing data and is available to the listing owner and participants in a conversation about that listing. Please obtain permission before sharing a reference’s contact information.

Service providers

EuroPetStay uses Supabase for authentication, data storage, and image storage. Google processes sign-in when you choose Google. The website also requests fonts from Google Fonts. These providers process technical information as needed to provide their services; their own privacy policies apply to that processing.

Retention and your choices

Information is kept while needed to provide the service and handle legitimate operational needs. Private identity documents and reference details remain stored until a deletion request is processed. Account removal may not automatically remove uploaded storage objects, so include those documents in any deletion request. To request access, correction, or deletion of your account information, email us. We may need to retain limited information where required by law or to resolve a security or service issue.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice as the service changes. Questions about privacy? Email us.
