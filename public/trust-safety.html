Trust & Safety

EuroPetStay helps owners and sitters find one another and communicate. Please discuss the arrangement and make your own decision before confirming a stay.

What the service provides

  • In-app messaging between the participants in a conversation.
  • Structured listing fields for pet needs, veterinary contacts, emergency contacts, dates, and agreed veterinary costs. Veterinary and emergency contact details are private to the listing owner and conversation participants, not shown on public listing cards.
  • A private ID-document upload field for sitters. Documents and reference contact details are not public.
  • Reviews for completed stays after the listing is marked complete and the end date has passed.

Important: EuroPetStay does not currently have an in-app ID review or background-check process. Uploading an ID document does not verify identity, and a profile should not be considered verified unless the platform separately confirms it. We do not provide pet insurance or guarantee the conduct of users, veterinary outcomes, or a backup sitter.

Before confirming a stay

Talk through the pet’s routine and medical needs, review the veterinary and emergency plan, confirm dates and responsibilities, and agree who pays veterinary costs. Keep a backup plan in case either person has to cancel. EuroPetStay does not charge for or arrange payment for the accommodation exchange.

Insurance and cancellation

EuroPetStay does not provide pet insurance. Veterinary costs are paid by the owner unless the participants agree otherwise. If a sitter cancels, we may not be able to find a replacement and cannot guarantee a backup; always have a separate plan.
