The service

EuroPetStay helps pet owners and sitters discover one another and communicate about stays. A stay is an arrangement between the owner and sitter; EuroPetStay does not charge for or arrange payment for the stay.

Accounts and conduct

Provide accurate account and listing information, use an account you are authorized to access, and keep your sign-in details secure. Use the service lawfully and respectfully. Do not post misleading, unlawful, or harmful content, or use another person’s information or property without permission.

Arrangements and pet care

Before confirming a stay, owners and sitters should discuss dates, responsibilities, emergency contacts, and the pets’ needs. Each person is responsible for deciding whether an arrangement is suitable and for complying with applicable laws. EuroPetStay does not verify every user or listing and cannot guarantee the conduct of users, the accuracy of listings, or the outcome of an arrangement.

You are responsible for content you submit and must have permission to share it. Listings and photos may be shown publicly; messages are intended for the conversation participants. Reviews are not currently available.
